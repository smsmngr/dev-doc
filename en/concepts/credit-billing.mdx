SmsManager deducts credit **only at the moment a message is sent**. It is not charged when you schedule a message for later, nor when you prepare a message in the admin interface. Understanding this is important for knowing when your account balance actually changes, and why a send can be scheduled even without sufficient credit.

---

## When credit is deducted

Credit is charged at **a single moment — when the message is sent**. At the very same time, SmsManager runs the final check that the message may be sent (for example, that the recipient is not on the blacklist or has not opted out).

Credit is **not** deducted:

- **when a send is scheduled** — a message with a future `datetime` is queued regardless of your current balance,
- **while preparing a message in the admin interface** — until the message is actually sent, your credit stays untouched.

<Note>
  Because credit is only reserved at send time, **a send is scheduled even if you don't have enough credit at that moment.** It is enough to top up any time before the message is sent.
</Note>

---

## The credit check at send time

Only at the moment of sending does SmsManager verify that you have enough credit for the given message, and if so, deducts it. The other send-time rules (blacklist, opt-out, etc.) are evaluated at the same point.

The amount deducted follows the message price based on destination, channel, and number of segments. For details on how SmsManager accepts and then processes a request, see the [Request acceptance](/concepts/request-acceptance) concept.

---

## Insufficient credit — error 307

If there isn't enough credit on the account at send time, the message is **rejected with error `[307] Insufficient credit`**. The message ends in the `rejected` state, and you receive the information at your callback URL:

```json Webhook event (excerpt)
{
  "message_id": "e27ff0ac-87b5-4e1d-b644-5fc6029e2a11",
  "result": "rejected",
  "result_info": "[307] Insufficient credit"
}
```

<Warning>
  **The send is not retried after error 307.** The message stays permanently in the `rejected` state — topping up credit does not automatically resend it. To deliver it, send it again as a new request once your credit is topped up.
</Warning>

---

## What this means for your integration

- **Scheduling does not guarantee sending.** Even a scheduled message must be covered by credit right up to the moment it is sent.
- **Watch your balance.** Especially for sends scheduled into the future, make sure there is enough credit before that time.
- **Handle the `rejected` state with code 307.** Listen on your callback URL and distinguish `[307] Insufficient credit` from other rejection causes in the `result_info` field. Resend rejected messages after topping up if needed.

<Tip>
  You can monitor your current balance and set up low-credit notifications via the REST API [Credit](/api-reference/rest/overview) section.
</Tip>
