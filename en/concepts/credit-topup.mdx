You can top up credit by **online bank transfer** or **payment card**. If you send larger volumes, you can automate top-ups or switch to monthly invoicing.

For when credit is deducted and what happens when it runs out, see the [Credit and billing](/concepts/credit-billing) concept.

---

## Recommendations for larger volumes

If you send a large number of messages, we recommend that you:

1. **Set up a recurring payment.** The variable symbol for payments never changes, so you don't need to generate top-up requests manually.
2. **Set up automatic top-up** when credit drops below 10 %. See the steps below.
3. **Switch to monthly invoicing** for sent messages. See the steps below.

---

## Online bank transfer

If your bank supports instant payments, we receive your payment in about 10 minutes, often faster. You can then start sending messages almost immediately.

### Credit was not topped up

If your credit did not increase after a payment, email us at [cc@smsmanager.cz](mailto:cc@smsmanager.cz). Include at least:

1. The variable symbol of your payment.
2. Which of our accounts you sent the payment to, and when.
3. Your bank account number.

A payment confirmation is not required, but it can help.

<Warning>
  Without these details, we are unable to trace your payment.
</Warning>

## Payment card

You can also top up credit online with a payment card. For new accounts, you may need to ask support to activate this option.

<Note>
  Payments with a corporate payment card may incur a percentage surcharge.
</Note>

### Activating card top-ups

1. Fill in all contact details in your account settings.
2. Contact support through the **online chat** in the admin and ask for activation.

---

## Automatic top-up by payment card

If you don't want to monitor and top up credit manually, set up automatic top-up. Your credit is topped up to 100 % of the original amount whenever it drops below 10 %.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Top up credit and enable automatic top-up">
    Enter the top-up amount, for example CZK 2,000. Before continuing to the payment gateway, check **Automatic top-up**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Credit tops itself up">
    Whenever your credit drops below 10 % of the topped-up amount (below CZK 200 in this example), the original amount of CZK 2,000 is topped up automatically.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You cannot change the 10 % threshold manually. If you want credit to be topped up sooner, increase the top-up amount. For example, with a top-up of CZK 5,000, the next top-up happens whenever credit drops below CZK 500.

## Monthly invoicing (postpaid)

If you send more messages, or topping up by transfer or card is impractical for you, you can switch to a **postpaid** account. You then pay for sent messages based on a monthly invoice.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Submit a request">
    Fill in the form to request a postpaid contract.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Receive the draft contract">
    You receive the prepared draft contract by email as a PDF.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Sign the contract online">
    We sign the contract online. All you need is a mobile phone number and an email address.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Pay the deposit">
    You receive a request to pay the deposit.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Switch to postpaid">
    We switch your account to postpaid the following day. You receive the invoice for sending the following month. The first invoice also deducts your remaining credit balance.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Deposit and sending limit

- The **deposit** serves as security. After 6 months, you can ask for it to be returned.
- You can usually send messages **up to three times the deposit amount**. This is an indicative limit. If you exceed it, we will contact you to agree on next steps.

<Tip>
  An AI assistant connected through the [MCP server](/ai/mcp-server) can check your credit balance with the `get_credit` tool and prepare bank transfer details with `get_topup_details`.
</Tip>
